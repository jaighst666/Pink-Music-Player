# Pink Music Player 💗🎧

A soft pink Android music player app idea.

## App Idea

Pink Music Player is a cute, cozy music player for Android with a soft pink theme, rounded buttons, hearts, and a simple layout.

## Planned Features

- Play music from phone storage
- Pause, skip, and go back
- Cute pink music player screen
- Album cover display
- Song title and artist
- Favorites/heart button
- Soft rounded UI

## Design Style

- Soft pink background
- White rounded cards
- Heart icons
- Kawaii/cozy look
- Simple and beginner-friendly
